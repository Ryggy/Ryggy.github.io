My Background / About Me
I’m a gameplay programmer based in Brisbane, Australia, fascinated by the hidden systems that make games feel alive. Having recently completed a Bachelor of Games Development (Programming) at SAE Institute, I specialise in building responsive mechanics, intelligent AI behaviours, and development tools that turn ambitious ideas into playable experiences.
I’m driven by curiosity and a desire to understand how small technical choices ripple outward to shape player decisions, emotions, and stories. I enjoy the challenge of solving complex problems with elegant, maintainable code and thrive in collaborative environments where creative and technical thinking come together.
Outside of development, my love of strategy games, modding, and tabletop design fuels my interest in emergent gameplay and systemic storytelling. I’m constantly exploring new technologies, experimenting with new ideas, and pushing myself to craft systems that surprise, challenge, and delight players.
