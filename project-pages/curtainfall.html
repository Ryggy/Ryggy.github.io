Curtainfall - Gothic Circus Roguelike
Role: Lead Programmer
Team Size: 6 | Keywords: Unity (C#), FMOD, Timeline | Platform: PC
Overview
Curtainfall is a gothic circus–themed roguelike developed as my final capstone project at SAE.
The game blends fast-paced combat, replayable mission structures, and atmospheric worldbuilding inside the grounds of a decaying circus. Players fight through interconnected arenas, facing twisted performers and minibosses as they uncover the story behind the fall of the curtain.
Check it out on Steam! View project on Gitlab
My Contributions
  • Designed and implemented the Mission System, Zone framework, and Object Pooling architecture.
  • Integrated FMOD for adaptive audio transitions and event-driven SFX.
  • Built UI functionality for mission tracking and settings.
  • Created data-driven tools for designers using ScriptableObjects.
