Sword in the Stone - Unreal Engine Scene Composition
Role: Environment Artist / Technical Designer
Team Size: Solo | Keywords: Unreal Engine, Lighting, Composition Study, Environment
Overview
Sword in the Stone is a cinematic environment project focused on scene composition, materials, and lighting within Unreal Engine 5.
The project explores how lighting and atmosphere can be used to guide player focus and evoke emotion. Inspired by Arthurian legend, the scene depicts a fantasy lakeside environment where rays of light illuminate a statue of the Lady of the Lake and the legendary sword in the stone beneath. The goal was to master environmental storytelling through composition, achieving a striking focal point and balanced visual harmony
My Contributions
  • Concept & Composition: Developed the scene concept and composition plan using Miro boards and lighting references, establishing a clear focal flow from light rays to the statue and sword.
  • Environment Creation: Built the terrain with Unreal’s Landscape Tool, applied procedural blend materials, and populated the scene using Quixel Megascans assets for natural realism.
  • Lighting & Visual Effects: Designed cinematic lighting with HDRI backdrops, volumetric fog, and custom material-based god rays. Added magical ambient effects using modified Niagara particles.
  • Cinematics & Rendering: Created a tracked camera sequence and adjusted render settings for high-quality output, including temporal sampling and increased screen percentage for sharpness.
Tools & Technologies
Unreal Engine 5, Quixel Megascans, Niagara VFX, HDRI Lighting, Post Process Volumes, Sequencer
Focus
