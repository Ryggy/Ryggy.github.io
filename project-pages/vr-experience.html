Liminal VR — Calm Experience Series
Role: Programmer
Team Size: 5 | Keywords: Unity (C#), VR, Timeline, Particle System | Platform: Liminal Portal (Meta Quest)
Overview
Created during my internship at Liminal VR, this project was designed as a Calm Experience for the Liminal Portal - a VR platform focused on mood-shaping short experiences.
The player finds themselves in a serene gallery-like environment where each exhibit transforms into an interactive, sensory meditation inspired by the natural elements: Fire, Water, Earth, and Air.
Each scene invites players to interact with the central artwork through hand movements, manipulating particles and visual effects in real time. These gestures influence the artwork’s form, lighting, and accompanying soundscape, creating a meditative feedback loop between player and environment.

My Contributions
  • Developed interactive flow-field particle systems responsive to real-time input.
  • Implemented transitions using Unity Timeline, synchronising lighting and audio between elemental environments.
  • Collaborated with designers and artists to ensure cohesive visual and emotional tone across all four experiences.
  • Ensured consistent framerate and performance within VR limitations, optimising particle counts, lighting, and materials for headset hardware.
